How it works

Scan the barcode or the label

Barcodes are looked up in a product database. If a product isn't there, photograph the ingredients list and Zesti reads it for you.

A score you can explain

Every result shows why it scored the way it did, ingredient by ingredient, and where the evidence comes from. The method is published inside the app.

Your own allergens and preferences

Save the allergens you avoid and Zesti flags them. Personal flags never change the overall score.

Zesti provides general information about food ingredients. It is not medical or dietary advice, and it is no substitute for reading the label if you have a food allergy.
