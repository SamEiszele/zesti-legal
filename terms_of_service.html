The Service

Zesti is a food ingredient scanning app that provides general information about food additives and ingredients based on published research and regulatory guidelines. Zesti is a product of Zesti Health (ABN 59 695 275 152), a sole trader business based in Queensland, Australia.

Zesti offers two ways to scan a product: reading its barcode, or photographing its printed ingredients list. Photos taken for label scanning are sent to a third-party AI service to extract the ingredient text, as described in our Privacy Policy.
