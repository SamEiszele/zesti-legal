Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Effective: April 2026

Zesti is built on the principle that you should know what's in your food — and what's in our app. This policy explains clearly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it's used. We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. Your health preferences stay private.

Who We Are

Zesti Health is a registered business name (ABN 59 695 275 152) operating under the Zesti Health trading name (ABN 59 695 275 152), Queensland, Australia. For privacy enquiries, contact us at privacy@zesti.app.

We are subject to the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We use Supabase for authentication — your password is never stored in plain text.

Health preferences

You can optionally enable health preferences (gut health focus, allergen alerts, synthetic colours, artificial sweeteners). These are stored as anonymous preference toggles — not medical conditions. No diagnosis or health condition is ever stored or inferred.

Scan history

When you scan a product, we store the product name, brand, scan date, and the ingredient score result. This is used to show your scan history within the app. We do not store images of food labels.

Device and usage data

We collect basic app usage data (screens visited, features used) to improve the app. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you individually. We do not use advertising trackers.

We do not collect: your location, contacts, financial information, biometric data, or any information about your actual health conditions or medical history.

How We Use Your Data
  • To provide the Zesti app and its scanning features
  • To personalise ingredient scores based on your health preferences
  • To maintain your scan history across devices
  • To process your subscription via RevenueCat (if you subscribe to Zesti Pro)
  • To respond to support enquiries
  • To improve the app based on aggregated usage patterns

We never use your data to:

  • Sell to third parties
  • Target you with advertising
  • Build a profile for marketing purposes
  • Make automated decisions about your health
Third Party Services

Supabase

We use Supabase (hosted in ap-southeast-2, Sydney) for our database and authentication. Your data is stored in Australia. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

RevenueCat

If you subscribe to Zesti Pro, your subscription is processed via RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your Apple ID transaction information to validate your subscription. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple App Store

Zesti is distributed via the Apple App Store. Apple's data practices are governed by Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/privacy.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data including your scan history, health preferences, and account information is permanently deleted within 30 days. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in the app.

Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act and Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of any non-essential data collection
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au
Health Information Disclaimer

Zesti provides general information about food additives and ingredients based on published research. This is not medical advice. Ingredient scores are for informational purposes only and should not be used to diagnose, treat, or manage any health condition. Always consult a qualified health professional for personal health decisions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use of Zesti after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
